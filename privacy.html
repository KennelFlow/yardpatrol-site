Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 27, 2026

Overview

Yard Patrol Pet Waste Removal provides business-management tools and a customer service portal. The current 1.0 release primarily stores business records locally on the owner device. Customer Mode in the next app update adds secure portal syncing so approved customers can view their own service information from their devices.

Information handled by the app and website

Yard Patrol may handle names, service addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, dog information, service plans, appointment and job information, service notes, invoices, payment records, service photos, customer requests, expenses, and business records. The website service-request form sends the information a prospective customer submits to Yard Patrol so the business can respond to the request.

Customer portal data

When an owner creates Customer App Access, the app securely sends that customer's portal information to Yard Patrol's hosted service. Portal data can include the customer's contact and service information, dogs, scheduled and completed jobs, before-and-after service photos, invoices, payment history, and customer service requests. Access is limited using a private customer access code. The server stores a one-way hash of the access code rather than the plain access code itself.

Square payments

When a customer or business user chooses to make or collect an electronic invoice payment with Square, Yard Patrol sends the invoice number, payment amount, customer name, and any optional customer email address or phone number needed to prepare checkout to the payment service and Square. Payment card details are entered on Square hosted checkout and are not stored by Yard Patrol.

Photos, backups, and exports

Owner-side JSON backups and CSV exports remain under the user's control. Service photos normally remain local unless the owner creates or syncs Customer App Access for that customer, in which case portal service photos may be securely uploaded so that customer can view them.

Tracking and advertising

Yard Patrol does not sell personal information and does not use customer information for third-party advertising or cross-app tracking.

Security and retention

Yard Patrol uses HTTPS for payment and customer-portal communication. Local records remain on the device until edited, deleted, restored, or the app and its local data are removed. Hosted customer-portal and service-request records are retained as needed to provide service and manage customer access. Square transaction information is also subject to Square's retention requirements and policies.
