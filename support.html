Yard Patrol Support

Use this page for help with the Yard Patrol Pet Waste Removal app and website.

App troubleshooting

  • Make sure you are running the newest Yard Patrol version available from the App Store or TestFlight.
  • For camera/photo features, allow Yard Patrol access when iOS asks for permission.
  • For service reminders, allow notifications in iPhone or iPad Settings.
  • If a backup is being restored, keep the app open until the restore finishes.

Square invoice payments

Electronic invoice payments use Square secure hosted checkout. Card information is entered with Square and is not stored inside Yard Patrol. After checkout, return to Yard Patrol and verify the Square payment before the invoice is marked paid.

Customer Mode

Customer Mode is included in Build 10, the next Yard Patrol app update. Approved customers receive a private access code from Yard Patrol. The customer side includes upcoming service, schedule-change and add-on requests, completed-service photos, invoices, Square payments, payment history, and service notifications. Customer access credentials are stored securely in the iOS Keychain on the device.

Data and backups

The owner/admin side keeps its primary business database locally and supports JSON backup and CSV export. When Customer App Access is enabled for a customer, the customer's portal information is securely synced so that customer can view it from their own device. Working customer and owner portal credentials are kept out of Yard Patrol JSON backups and stored in the iOS Keychain.

App Store status

Yard Patrol 1.0 is currently with Apple for review. Build 10 adds Customer Mode, has been uploaded to TestFlight for testing, and will be released as the next app update.
