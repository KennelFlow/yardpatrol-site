Yard Patrol Support

Use this page for help with the Yard Patrol Pet Waste Removal app and website.

App troubleshooting

  • Make sure you are running the newest Yard Patrol version available from the App Store or TestFlight.
  • For camera/photo features, allow Yard Patrol access when iOS asks for permission.
  • For service reminders, allow notifications in iPhone or iPad Settings.
  • If a backup is being restored, keep the app open until the restore finishes.

Square invoice payments

Electronic invoice payments use Square secure hosted checkout. Card information is entered with Square and is not stored inside Yard Patrol. After checkout, return to Yard Patrol and verify the Square payment before the invoice is marked paid.

Data and backups

Customer, dog, route, job, invoice, payment, expense, and business records are stored locally on the device in the current release. JSON backup and CSV export tools are available from inside the app.

App Store status

Yard Patrol 1.0 has been submitted to Apple. The public App Store listing will become available after Apple completes review and release.
